# Foundational Axiomatic Map (Super-Intelligence Edition)
## Core Laws for General Scientific and Engineering Intelligence

This map is the primary reference for the model's "Universal Reasoning." The model must treat these not as facts to remember, but as operators to apply.

### 1. Mathematics: The Logic of Structure
#### Linear Algebra & Tensor Analysis
- **Multilinear Algebra**: Tensor products, Contraction, and the Einstein Summation Convention.
- **Advanced Operators**: Spectral Theory, Singular Value Decomposition (SVD), and Moore-Penrose Pseudoinverse.
- **Differential Geometry**: Manifolds, Metric Tensors ($g_{\mu\nu}$), Christoffel Symbols, and Ricci Curvature (The language of General Relativity).

#### Analysis & Topology
- **Complex Analysis**: Cauchy-Riemann equations, Residue Theorem, and Analytic Continuation.
- **Functional Analysis**: Hilbert Spaces, Banach Spaces, and Operator Theory.
- **Topology**: Homeomorphisms, Homotopy, and the study of invariants in manifold shapes.

#### Probability & Information Theory
- **Measure Theory**: Lebesgue integration and the foundations of probability.
- **Stochastic Calculus**: Ito's Lemma, Brownian Motion, and the Fokker-Planck Equation.
- **Information Theory**: Shannon Entropy, Kullback-Leibler (KL) Divergence, and Mutual Information.

### 2. Physics: The Laws of the Universe
#### Fluid Dynamics & Continuum Mechanics
- **Navier-Stokes Equations**: The fundamental equations for viscous fluid flow.
- **Euler Equations**: Incompressible and compressible flow without viscosity.
- **Turbulence Theory**: Reynolds-averaged Navier-Stokes (RANS) and Kolmogorov scales.
- **Conservation Laws**: Mass (Continuity), Momentum, and Energy conservation.

#### Quantum Mechanics & Computing
- **Quantum State Space**: Bra-ket notation ($\langle \psi | \phi \rangle$), State vectors, and Density Matrices.
- **Dynamics**: The Schrödinger Equation (Time-dependent and Independent) and the Heisenberg Picture.
- **Quantum Information**: Qubit gates (Hadamard, CNOT, Pauli), Quantum Fourier Transform, and Shor's/Grover's algorithms.
- **Field Theory**: The transition from Quantum Mechanics to Quantum Field Theory (QFT).

#### Thermodynamics & Statistical Mechanics
- **The Laws of Thermodynamics**: Entropy, Enthalpy, and the Second Law.
- **Statistical Ensembles**: Microcanonical, Canonical, and Grand Canonical ensembles.
- **Phase Transitions**: Order parameters, Critical exponents, and the Ising Model.

### 3. Molecular & Chemical Engineering
#### Protein Architecture & Proteomics
- **Primary to Quaternary Structure**: Alpha-helices, Beta-sheets, and the folding problem.
- **Thermodynamics of Folding**: Hydrophobic effect, Hydrogen bonding, and Van der Waals forces.
- **Molecular Dynamics**: Force fields (CHARMM, AMBER) and the simulation of molecular trajectories.

#### Chemical Kinetics & Synthesis
- **Reaction Dynamics**: Transition State Theory and the Arrhenius Equation.
- **Stoichiometry**: Mass balance and reaction equilibrium.
- **Computational Chemistry**: Density Functional Theory (DFT) and the Hartree-Fock method.
